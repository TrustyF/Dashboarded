Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Dashboarded is a personal, single-user home dashboard application. It is not a public service, has no other users, and is not distributed to or used by anyone other than its developer.

What data is accessed

The app connects to the developer's own Google account to read:

  • Google Calendar events (read-only), to display upcoming events on the dashboard.
  • Google Health metrics and activity data (read-only), to display health and fitness statistics on the dashboard.

How the data is used and stored

Data is fetched directly from Google's APIs and displayed on the dashboard in real time or cached briefly (up to one hour) to reduce API calls. It is never sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party, and is not used for advertising or analytics. All data stays on hardware controlled by the developer.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
